The platform

Everything ParentSquare does for a district — rebuilt for a program that isn't one.

Districts get a student information system to lean on. Beyond Limits doesn't. So the identifier, the roster, the agreement, the message and the attendance rule all live here, in one place, under your own name.

Core 01

Family Records & Participant Codes

One row per family, however many programs they join and however many times they sign. The list is the only place a code is ever minted — so the calendar, the messages and ParentSquare all point at the same stable identity. Names, birthdays, addresses, language and income stay in one locked ledger with a full change history.

Comms 02

Messaging that reaches every family

Text, email, voice and in-app in one send — auto-translated into each family's language, with no login, no app and no password to forget. Scheduled reminders, RSVPs, read receipts and a per-family delivery trail.

Automation 03

Missed-session recovery

Rules you write in plain language — "if a scholar misses 2 of 5 sessions, text the family; at 4, call; at 5, the tutor gets a check-in task." Nothing fires twice, nothing gets forgotten.

Intake 04

Onboarding & agreements

A QR code, a warm three-minute orientation, a signed agreement, a code — in one flow. One signature per program, never a re-submission to move tracks.

Operations 05

Attendance & sessions

Check a scholar in by typing four digits of their code. Track streaks, no-shows, room capacity and tutor hours — then turn all of it into the numbers a grant application actually wants.

Migration 06

Out to ParentSquare, cleanly

Remind is folding into ParentSquare and your families aren't in a district, so the school-assigned number isn't available. Your participant code is the identifier — export it in ParentSquare's import shape, validate every row before you upload, and keep your own copy authoritative.
