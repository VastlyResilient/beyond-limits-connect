What a family actually sees

No app. No password. No login wall.

A text arrives, the page opens in the family's own language, and everything that needs a decision fits on one thumb. That is the whole design brief.

  • Auto-translated into the language the family chose — 190+ available, four in use today.
  • RSVP and permission slips in two taps, with a receipt the office can see.
  • The family's participant code shown right on the home screen, so nobody has to hunt for it.
  • Nothing student-facing. Parents and guardians only — no chat window for children to poke at.
  • Attendance nudges arrive as a friendly note with a reschedule button, not a warning.
